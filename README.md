EMBERDEEP: Delve of the Hollow Lantern is an original roguelite dungeon
crawler about light. You are the delver who carries the last lantern into
the sunken hollow: three floors (the Ember Warrens, the Fungal Deep, the
Hollow Throne), six rooms a floor, nine kinds of foe, three warden bosses,
20 draftable relics, shop rooms, a heart-trading altar, treasure chests,
elite foes, and a lantern whose light is your sight — the dark presses in
around it.

Clear a room to open its doors. Fork rooms offer a choice: north, the elite
— south, the treasure. Foes drop ember shards; fill the bar to draft 1 of 3
relics. Slay the Hollow King on floor 3 to win — "The Hollow is lit" — and
unlock Endless Delve, where the floors keep coming and the dark grows
hungrier.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow
keys, aim with the mouse, hold click to fire lantern-bolts, dash with Shift
or Space or right-click, [1] Kindle Burst, [2] Ember Ward, P pause, M mute,
R restart.
